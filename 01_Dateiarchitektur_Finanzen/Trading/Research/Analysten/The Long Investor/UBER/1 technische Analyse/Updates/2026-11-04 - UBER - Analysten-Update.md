**Originaltext:**

> $UBER saved by the invalidation line and now back to the 0.38 fib at $70.90 already after testing $64
> 
> No changes to the chart and Earnings are just out:
> 
> $UBER reported Q3 before the open today: bookings and profit beat or matched, but the Q4 EPS guide is what the market is chewing on.
> 
> Headline
> 
> * Revenue was $14.77B, up 10%, against about $14.69B expected.
> 
> * Non-GAAP EPS was $0.88, up 35%, against $0.86 expected.
> 
> * GAAP EPS was $0.25, down from $3.11 a year ago. Last year included a $4.9B tax benefit, and this quarter had a $476M loss from revaluing equity investments.
> 
> * Revenue by segment: Mobility $7.52B (down 2%, a miss), Delivery $5.49B (up 23%), Freight $1.76B (up 34%, a big beat).
> 
> Bookings
> 
> * Gross bookings were $59.82B, up 20%, in line with expectations.
> 
> * Mobility bookings were $29.51B, up 18%, a little light, with the Middle East conflict costing about 0.75 points.
> 
> * Delivery bookings were $28.54B, up 22%, and Freight bookings were $1.77B, up 35%.
> 
> * There were 4.06B trips, up 16%, and 215M monthly active consumers, up 14%.
> 
> Profit and cash
> 
> * Adjusted EBITDA was $2.96B, up 31%, or 5.0% of gross bookings versus 4.5% a year ago.
> 
> * Free cash flow was $2.54B, up 14%, and cash was $10.1B.
> 
> * I couldn't find the Q3 buyback amount.
> 
> Q4 guidance
> 
> * Gross bookings are guided at $63.25B to $65.25B, up 18% to 22%, with a midpoint in line with consensus.
> 
> * Adjusted EBITDA is guided at $3.13B to $3.23B, against $3.15B expected.
> 
> * Non-GAAP EPS is guided at $0.95 to $0.99, against about $1.06 expected. This is the weak spot, though one source calls it in line at the midpoint.
> 
> Management commentary
> 
> * International Mobility came in slightly below expectations because of high fuel prices and tighter driver supply, and Uber is investing in driver supply.
> 
> * About 700 driverless vehicles are expected on the platform by year-end, and more than 2,000 by the end of 2027.
> 
> * The Costco delivery partnership is tracking ahead of plan.
> 
> Stock
> 
> * $UBER was up about 3% to 4% intraday, with a high of $69.50, helped by a broad post-midterms rally. I couldn't find the Nov 3 close, and the last one I saw was $66.71 on Nov 2.
> 
> * The average Street target is about $101 to $103, with 27 Buys and 3 Holds.
