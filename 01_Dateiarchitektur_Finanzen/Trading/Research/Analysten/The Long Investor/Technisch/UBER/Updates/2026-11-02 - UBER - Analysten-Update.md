## Originalbeitrag
> $UBER is at $67.50 and the weekly chart is sitting right on the 200 WMA in Wave 4, with earnings coming up on Wednesday.
> 
> Q3 results are on Nov 4 before the open. The Street is looking for about $0.86 to $0.90 in EPS, revenue near $14.7B and gross bookings of about $59.8B, against company guidance of $58.25B to $60.25B.
> 
> So why has it fallen from $100 to here? The big worry is robotaxis, because $GOOGs Waymo wound down its Phoenix deal in June and keeps growing on its own app, and $TSLA is pushing the same way.
> 
> On top of that $UBER plans to spend more than $10B on self driving cars and infrastructure, which breaks the asset light model that investors loved.
> 
> The August report didn't help, with Q3 profit guidance below estimates, trip growth slowing in Brazil, and buybacks of just $518M in the quarter.
> 
> Add in the fear around $DASH buying $CART and you get a stock down -28% from its high while the S&P is up 13%.
> 
> But the business is not broken. Q2 gross bookings grew 24% to $58B, EPS beat, and it sits at about 14 times trailing earnings with roughly 73% of US ride hailing.
> 
> On the Street, 58 analysts are about 86% Buy with just one Sell, and the average target is near $103, around 50% above here.
> 
> For me the chart is fairly straightforward.. this is a classic Wave 4 pull back into the 200 WMA, and the Wave 1 high near $64 is our invalidation line, because Wave 4 can not infringe upon Wave 1.
> 
> Bulls want to see us flip the 0.38 Fib at $70.90 back to support, which is our Bull Case.
> 
> If we lose the 200 WMA, the 0.5 Fib at $61.22 and the 0.618 Fib at $51.55 are the next levels down.
> 
> Above that, Wave 5 targets the 1.618 Fib at $100.79 and the 2.618 Fib at $151.04.
> 
> Earnings can swing this either way, so let's be greedy for support confirmation.
