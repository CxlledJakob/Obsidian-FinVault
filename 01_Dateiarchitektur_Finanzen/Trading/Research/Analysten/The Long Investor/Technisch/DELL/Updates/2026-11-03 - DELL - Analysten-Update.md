## Originalbeitrag
> $DELL closed Monday at $393 after a brutal run and testing the 0.38 Fib which is typically where Wave A completes, the next earnings are on Tue Nov 24 after the close,
> 
> The Street is looking for EPS of about $6.68 on revenue of roughly $50.15B, while the company's own guide of $49B and $6.50 is actually lower than last quarter's $7.04, which is part of the worry.
> 
> So why the drop from nearly $595? It's been a stack of hits, starting Oct 13 when the CEO said the memory shortage could last around 4 years.
> 
> The next day a downgrade on enterprise AI timing knocked it down -11%, with the argument that revenue is more about memory costs than AI demand.
> 
> Then on Oct 30 it fell almost -11% after $INTC said customers are slowing PC purchases because of memory prices, and expects PC shipments to fall 12% this year.
> 
> On Monday it lost another -7.4% in a chip selloff, with $INTC, $AMD and $MU all down, and that was four straight down days.
> 
> Inventory has tripled to $21.3B from $7.2B a year ago, which is the other number the bears keep pointing at.
> 
> BUT Wave 5 hot the 4.618 Fib and NO price action gets above this fib without an ABC pull back, it was clear to us.
> 
> But there is an opportunity soon, look at what the business is doing.. a $95B AI backlog, a $74B AI server sales target for the year, and Q2 revenue up +58% with EPS of $7.04 against about $5 expected.
> 
> The Street average target is about $595 from 29 analysts, with at least 20 Buy ratings, and the highest target is $735, so the analysts have not given up.
> 
> For me the chart is fairly straightforward.. Wave 5 topped right at the 4.618 Fib at $592.82, which is where our SELL label sat, and we've been falling out of it ever since.
> 
> Now price is sitting on the 0.382 Fib at $393 so the Buy Zone starts right here.
> 
> The real support is lower, where the 0.5 Fib at $331 lines up with the rising 200 WMA, and that is the area I'm watching the closest.
> 
> The 0.618 Fib at $269 is the deepest level, and the Bull Case line way down at $180 is the big blue line.
> 
> Be greedy for support confirmation, watch for consolidation in the buy zone before adding.
